# Dharma

## Canonical Name

Dharma

## Sanskrit

धर्म

## Transliteration

Dharma

## Śabda

धर्म

## Dhātu

धृ (Dhṛ)

Meaning: To uphold, sustain, support.

## Vyākaraṇa

Derived from the verbal root धृ.

## Nirukta

That which upholds or sustains.

## Bīja

—

## Tattva

Principle of sustaining cosmic order.

## Śakti

Preservation and righteous order.

## Functional Meaning

Represents the sustaining principle governing righteous conduct and universal order.
