# Moksha

## Canonical Name

Moksha

## Sanskrit

मोक्ष

## Transliteration

Mokṣa

## Śabda

मोक्ष

## Dhātu

मुच् (Muc)

Meaning: To release.

## Vyākaraṇa

Derived from the root मुच्.

## Nirukta

Liberation.

## Bīja

—

## Tattva

Liberation

## Śakti

Freedom

## Functional Meaning

Freedom from ignorance and the cycle of birth and death.
